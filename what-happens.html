What to Expect When You Join Us

Most people want to know what they are walking into before they commit. Here is the honest version.

A Typical Outing

Arrival and check-in
Meet up, grab coffee, and settle in. No pressure to share your story. Come as you are.
Orientation and safety
We cover range rules, day-of expectations, and who you are teamed up with so the day stays safe and low-pressure.
Shared activity

Wrap-up

Before You Come

What it costs

Nothing, for veterans.

Outings are free for veterans. Public fundraiser events may have a posted entry fee, and veterans still get in free.

What to bring

Bring your own gear if you have it. If you are a recipient, bring the setup awarded at banquet or special distribution events. Wear closed-toe shoes and dress for the weather — outdoor events run rain or shine unless we call it off.

If you do not have gear yet, contact us before the event so we can plan next steps and connect you to upcoming award opportunities. Field points only, no broadheads.

Who runs it

Events are organized by Bows for Battle leaders and trusted host partners. Safety leads, experienced volunteers, and veteran peers keep each outing structured and welcoming throughout the year.

Bringing someone

Spouses, family and friends are welcome at range days. Tell us when you sign up so we can coordinate space with the host organization.

Accessibility

Adaptive equipment and seated options are available at every outing. Tell us what you need when you sign up and it will be set up before you arrive.

Ready to come out?

Check that you are eligible and put your name in. It takes two minutes.
